# Carer’s Circle membership, cancellation and refund draft

Status: **for Victoria, accountant and UK legal review — not customer-facing**  
Prepared: 2 September 2026

## Proposed customer journey

1. Before registration, show the full recurring price, whether VAT is included, the seven-day trial length, the date of the first payment, payment frequency, core benefits, cancellation method and refund/cooling-off information together.
2. Require an affirmative action to accept the membership terms and a separate choice for any optional marketing.
3. Send a durable confirmation containing the agreed terms and trial/payment dates.
4. Send a reminder before the trial converts to paid membership. The timing and contents require legal review.
5. Provide a simple online cancellation route that is no harder than joining.
6. Acknowledge cancellation in writing and state the effective date and access-end date.
7. Keep an auditable record of the accepted terms, reminder and cancellation acknowledgement.

## Draft cancellation wording

> You may ask to cancel your Carer’s Circle membership using the cancellation control in your account or the published membership-support contact. We will acknowledge your request in writing and confirm when charging and access will end. We will not charge a cancellation fee. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Do not publish “zero contract” or “cancel anytime” until the following have been confirmed:

- whether cancellation stops the next renewal or ends access immediately;
- treatment of a cancellation during the proposed trial;
- treatment of access already supplied during a statutory cooling-off period;
- whether any fixed-fee service purchased separately has different cancellation rules;
- the inbox or account control used to cancel; and
- the response time for cancellation acknowledgements.

## Draft refund framework

The final policy should never exclude statutory rights. Subject to professional review, the process should provide for:

- refunds required by applicable consumer law;
- prompt correction of duplicate or erroneous charges;
- refund of a payment taken after an effective cancellation;
- a documented review route for service failure or material misdescription; and
- written reasons where a discretionary refund is declined.

The policy must state the refund method and expected processing time. It must also explain any lawful effect of requesting immediate digital access during a cooling-off period. Those points cannot be finalised without Victoria’s commercial decision and legal review.

## Decisions still required

- final recurring price and currency;
- whether the displayed price includes VAT and the confirmed VAT position;
- whether payment details are collected at trial registration;
- exact first-payment date and time zone;
- one-trial-per-person eligibility and misuse controls;
- reminder timing;
- access-end rule following cancellation;
- discretionary refund circumstances; and
- approved cancellation contact and service hours.

## Legal-review notes

The current Consumer Contracts Regulations and Consumer Rights Act remain relevant. The subscription-contract provisions in the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 are expected to come into force in 2027 according to current GOV.UK guidance. The build should nevertheless be designed for clear pre-contract information, reminders, easy cancellation and durable acknowledgements. A reviewer should confirm the law and commencement position immediately before launch.
