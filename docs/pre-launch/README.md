# The Clara Path pre-launch governance pack

Status: **working drafts for Victoria and professional review — not published terms**  
Prepared: 2 September 2026

This folder contains the work that can be prepared without committing Victoria to a price, service level, refund position, professional claim or safeguarding role.

## Documents

- `membership-cancellation-and-refund-draft.md` — consumer-facing structure and operational checklist for the proposed membership and seven-day trial.
- `community-moderation-draft.md` — moderation, reporting, removal and commercial-content controls for Carer’s Circle.
- `safeguarding-and-enquiry-escalation-draft.md` — boundaries and a proposed internal response flow for enquiries and community reports.
- `victoria-decision-and-evidence-pack.md` — the short list of facts, evidence and decisions that only Victoria can provide.
- `implementation-readiness.md` — technical activation gates and the current safe state.

## Status boundary

These documents are operational and drafting aids. They are not legal advice and must not be presented as final customer terms. The final privacy notice, membership terms, cancellation/refund terms and safeguarding process require Victoria’s approval and review by a suitably qualified UK professional before launch.
