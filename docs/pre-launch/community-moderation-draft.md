# Carer’s Circle community moderation draft

Status: **operational draft for approval and UK legal review — not final rules**  
Prepared: 2 September 2026

## Purpose

Carer’s Circle is intended for general peer support, practical preparation and responsible signposting. It is not a case-management record, emergency channel, safeguarding reporting service, clinical service or legal-advice service.

## Member rules

Members must:

- use respectful, non-discriminatory language;
- keep posts general and remove identifying information;
- avoid names, addresses, NHS numbers, benefit references, financial details, passwords, health records, case notes and unredacted documents;
- avoid presenting personal experience as professional advice;
- avoid diagnosing, prescribing or instructing another person to stop treatment;
- avoid threats, harassment, impersonation, fraud and unlawful material;
- avoid unsolicited selling, recruitment, fundraising and affiliate links; and
- report concerning content rather than investigating or confronting another member.

## Moderation workflow

1. Record the report identifier, content identifier, reporter, time and stated reason.
2. Check first for immediate danger, credible threats, safeguarding indicators or exposed sensitive data.
3. Restrict or remove exposed personal data promptly where authorised.
4. Classify the report: privacy, safety, misinformation, harassment, commercial content, illegality or other rule breach.
5. Apply the least severe effective response: guidance, edit/request, removal, warning, temporary restriction or suspension.
6. Record the action and concise reason. Preserve only what is necessary for the moderation purpose.
7. Notify the member where appropriate, including how to ask for a review.
8. Escalate safety or legal uncertainty to the named lead; moderators do not investigate alleged abuse.

## Immediate-action categories

- credible risk of imminent harm;
- a child or adult at risk who may be in immediate danger;
- threats of violence or self-harm;
- exposed health records, financial credentials or authentication details;
- apparent criminal exploitation or fraud; and
- court-restricted or clearly unlawful content.

The response must follow the approved safeguarding procedure. The platform must not promise that a moderator is available continuously.

## Provider and product recommendations

- Member suggestions are submitted privately for review, not posted as endorsements.
- No paid placement, commission, affiliate link or supplier promotion is permitted until verification, conflicts, labelling, pricing, moderation and removal rules are approved.
- Any future commercial relationship must be labelled clearly next to the relevant content.
- Inclusion must never imply guaranteed quality, suitability, availability or outcome.

## Governance still required

Victoria must appoint moderators, set operating hours, approve the warning/suspension thresholds and name the safeguarding escalation lead. A UK reviewer should assess the community’s obligations, including privacy, consumer protection and any applicable online-safety duties, before public launch.
