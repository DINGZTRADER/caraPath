# Safeguarding and enquiry escalation draft

Status: **internal working procedure — not an emergency service and not yet active**  
Prepared: 2 September 2026

## Public boundary

The Clara Path provides information, preparation, advocacy and system-navigation support. It does not replace emergency services, NHS urgent care, a local-authority safeguarding team, the police or a regulated clinician.

Public wording should say:

> If someone is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent medical help that is not life-threatening, use NHS 111. Concerns that a child or adult at risk is being abused or neglected should be reported to the relevant local authority or police. This website is not monitored as an emergency or safeguarding reporting service.

## Proposed internal triage

1. **Read only what is necessary.** Do not request a full health record or case file through the initial enquiry form.
2. **Check immediate danger.** If the message suggests an immediate risk, direct the sender to 999 or the appropriate emergency route. Staff must not attempt an investigation.
3. **Identify the nation and local authority.** Safeguarding routes differ between England and Wales and are normally led locally.
4. **Escalate internally.** Send the minimum necessary information to the appointed safeguarding lead using the approved secure channel.
5. **Provide factual signposting.** Use the relevant local-authority safeguarding contact, police or NHS route. Do not promise a particular statutory response.
6. **Record proportionately.** Record the time, risk category, action and recipient. Do not duplicate sensitive information unnecessarily.
7. **Follow up within operating hours.** Never imply continuous monitoring unless that service is actually staffed.
8. **Review and delete.** Apply the approved retention schedule and securely remove records no longer required.

## Data-protection controls before opening enquiries

- identify the controller and publish a complete privacy notice at collection;
- document an Article 6 lawful basis and, if health information is processed, an appropriate Article 9 condition;
- collect only the minimum information required for triage;
- state recipients, retention, individual rights and complaint routes;
- secure data in transit and at rest with role-based access;
- separate optional marketing consent from the service enquiry;
- establish subject-access, correction, erasure and incident-response procedures; and
- assess whether a data-protection impact assessment is required.

## Decisions and evidence still required

- named safeguarding lead and deputy;
- staff training and supervision arrangements;
- approved secure escalation channel;
- England and Wales safeguarding contacts and out-of-hours handling;
- operating hours and response wording;
- lawful bases, special-category condition and retention schedule;
- data processor list and contracts; and
- ICO registration evidence where the business states that it is registered.

The enquiry form must remain closed until these operational and privacy controls are approved and implemented.
