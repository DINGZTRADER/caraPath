Welcome to the Carer’s Circle

Clarity, community and a practical way forward.

Supporting someone you love should not mean having to decipher every form, pathway and assessment alone. The Clara Path offers a calm, organised place to begin when the system feels hard to navigate.

When a loved one has physical, cognitive or mental-health needs, the demands of caring can become overwhelming. Paperwork, assessments and changing criteria across local authorities and the NHS can leave families feeling isolated and unsure what to do next.

The Carer’s Circle is built to make that journey less lonely. It brings together thoughtful navigation, useful preparation and responsible signposting, helping you identify relevant routes, understand the questions to ask and approach the next conversation with greater confidence.

We translate complex public information into clearer, practical preparation. Decisions about funding, care, treatment and legal matters remain with the appropriate authority or qualified professional.

“No judgement. No question is silly. A calmer next step can make a difficult system feel possible again.”

Carer’s Circle community standards

A safe, respectful space to learn and be heard.

Caring can be emotionally exhausting. These standards help protect every person who chooses to take part in the Carer’s Circle.

  1. 01
    Zero judgement. Maximum empathy.

    There is no place for shaming, blame or unkindness. Questions and experiences deserve to be met with care and respect.

  2. 02
    Protect personal privacy.

    Do not post names, addresses, dates of birth, reference numbers, barcodes or unredacted official documents.

  3. 03
    Information, not formal advice.

    Circle resources are for education, support and practical preparation. Clinical, legal and benefits decisions must be handled by the appropriate qualified professional or authority.

  4. 04
    Keep personal stories private.

    Do not screenshot, repost or share another member’s story, struggle or personal details outside the Circle.

  5. 05
    No unsolicited selling or pitching.

    Do not use the Circle to market private insurance, care agencies or independent services to other members.

Clear service boundaries

Supportive, careful and responsible.

The Clara Path complements existing health, social care, statutory and charitable support. We help carers prepare and navigate information; we do not replace regulated clinical, legal or statutory services.

  • Information and navigation support, not medical diagnosis, treatment or clinical advice.
  • Guidance and conversation preparation, not legal advice or statutory advocacy.
  • No guarantees about funding, equipment, eligibility, assessment outcomes or service availability.
  • Privacy-conscious support: do not share medical records, financial details or personally identifying information through public channels.
