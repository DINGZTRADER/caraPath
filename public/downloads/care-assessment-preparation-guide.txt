CARE ASSESSMENT PREPARATION GUIDE

Use this guide to organise your thoughts before a carer’s assessment.

1. YOUR CARING ROLE
- Who do you care for? Keep this general in your notes unless you are completing an official form.
- What practical tasks do you carry out?
- Roughly how much time does caring take each day or week?
- What happens overnight?

2. IMPACT ON YOU
- Sleep and rest
- Physical health
- Emotional wellbeing
- Work or education
- Relationships and family life
- Social contact and time for yourself

3. WHAT IS BECOMING DIFFICULT?
- Which parts of caring are hardest to sustain?
- Are there safety concerns?
- Are you missing appointments, work or rest because of caring?
- What support is already in place, and where are the gaps?

4. SUPPORT TO ASK ABOUT
- Breaks or respite
- Equipment or adaptations
- Direct Payments
- Carer support services
- Emergency or contingency planning
- Local voluntary-sector support

5. QUESTIONS FOR THE ASSESSOR
- What support may be available locally?
- What happens after the assessment?
- How will decisions be recorded?
- Who should I contact if my situation changes?

IMPORTANT
Describe your circumstances accurately, including how needs vary and what happens on difficult days. Do not exaggerate or minimise your situation.

This guide is for general preparation only. It does not replace legal, clinical, benefits or statutory advice and does not guarantee any assessment outcome.
