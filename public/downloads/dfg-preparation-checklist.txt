DISABLED FACILITIES GRANT (DFG) PREPARATION CHECKLIST

Use this checklist to organise your questions before approaching your Local Authority.

THE PRACTICAL PROBLEM
What is difficult or unsafe in the home?

THE ADAPTATION BEING CONSIDERED
- 
- 

HOW THE CURRENT HOME AFFECTS DAILY LIFE
- Access into or around the home
- Bathroom or washing facilities
- Essential rooms or facilities
- Safety or independence

PROFESSIONAL INFORMATION ALREADY AVAILABLE
- Occupational therapy assessment, if any
- Other relevant professional recommendations
- Existing adaptation history

QUESTIONS FOR THE LOCAL AUTHORITY
- How do I start the DFG process locally?
- Is an assessment required before works begin?
- What financial assessment applies in this case?
- What costs may or may not be covered?
- What happens if the proposed works cost more than the grant available?

IMPORTANT
Do not commit to building work on the assumption it will be funded. Check the current Local Authority process before proceeding. This checklist is general information only and does not guarantee eligibility or funding.
